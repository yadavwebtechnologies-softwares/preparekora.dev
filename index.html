PrepareKora Workspace

Create clean, professional question images for exams and study material.

PrepareKora Workspace is an offline-first authoring tool for preparing bilingual examination visuals. Compose direct-answer and four-option questions with clear explanations, live-preview each layout, and export ready-to-publish images.

Two question formats
Direct answer or four-option multiple choice with explanation.
Four image ratios
1:1, 4:3, 3:4 and 16:9 — the content adapts fluidly to every frame.
Automatic IDs
Structured identifiers per subject, generated without any manual entry.
Your workspace, your rules
Subject list, prefixes, brand text, QR destination — all adjustable.
